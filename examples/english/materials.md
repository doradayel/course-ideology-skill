# 大学英语输入材料：一次图书馆开放时间试行

本文件所有学校、人物、事件、调查和数字均为本项目原创的虚构教学材料，不是新闻报道或实测数据。段落号用于课堂引用。本例无需联网、原课件或现有思政元素库。

## 教师请求及本课条件

请用 `$course-ideology`，为大学英语“新闻摘要中的事实与观点区分”设计45分钟课程思政融入方案。对象为大学一年级学生，能够阅读下面短文和写简单英语句子，尚容易把个人观点改写成事实。没有原教案，请从这些文字设计完整流程，给出教师话术、学生任务和参考答案。

专业目标：区分可据材料核对的事实信息与观点；保留消息来源、范围、时间和不确定性；写一段55—70词的准确摘要。希望学生在表达中尊重证据和他人观点，不预设是否赞成延长开放时间。

## E1：Campus Library Notice and Student Comment

### P1

Qinghe College will keep its main library reading room open until 10 p.m. on weekdays for a four-week trial beginning next Monday. The change applies only to the reading room. Desk services will still close at 7 p.m.

### P2

The student office invited 1,200 students to complete a voluntary survey. It received 240 responses, and 168 respondents supported later opening hours. The notice does not describe the views of students who did not respond.

### P3

“We hope the trial will give students more choice,” said the library director. Staff will record room use and collect feedback during the trial. The college has not decided whether to make the change permanent.

### P4

One student, Mei, welcomed the plan. “I think this will definitely improve every student's grades,” she said. The notice provides no study of the plan's effect on grades. Another student asked whether the same funding could support more daytime seats.

## E2：A Draft Summary for Revision

Qinghe College has permanently extended all library services to 10 p.m. because 70% of its students demanded the change. The plan will improve every student's grades. Students agree that it is the best use of library funding.

## E3：对照句

1. The college has announced a four-week trial.
2. The trial is the best decision the college has ever made.
3. Mei said that the trial would improve every student's grades.
4. The trial will improve every student's grades.
5. Seventy percent of survey respondents supported later hours.
6. Seventy percent of all students supported later hours.
